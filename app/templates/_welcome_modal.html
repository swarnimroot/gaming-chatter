Gaming-Chatter

A weekly gaming brief, what is it?

The problem

Gaming news is scattered across feeds, subs, and channels. Generic Monday roundups read the same for everyone — they miss what one reader actually cares about.

pcgamer r/games yt: DF eurogamer +27 → ?
What this does

Daily ingest from 15 sites + 10 subreddits + 6 YouTube channels. Cluster the week’s items by topic. Opus writes a 9-card brief — biggest stories, hottest games, trends, risks, watch next week, more.
